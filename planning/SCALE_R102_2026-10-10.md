## Findings

- Row navigation remains about 5-7 ms and queued automatic saves about 93-110 ms across the sizes. No steep growth was observed in these two paths.
- Group opening is about 0.64-1.09 seconds. The 100-report groups are about 1.01-1.09 seconds.
- Report editor opening is the largest measured delay: median 2.44-4.01 seconds. Editor closes are about 36-48 ms. Next recommended work: instrument report editor initialization, section construction, control refresh and first layout before choosing an optimization.
- A separate EVAL profile isolates cache reload and unchanged average refresh costs:

| EVAL reports | Cache reload median (ms) | Average refresh median (ms) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 25 | 185.95 | 9.26 |
| 50 | 271.96 | 9.05 |
| 100 | 366.45 | 10.41 |

Cache construction scales with member count; average refresh costs about 9-10 ms here. Cache reload includes native binding/viewport work, so these are not pure query timings. Avoid prioritizing average optimization over the substantially larger editor-open delay.

## Verification and method

- Each group: 3 opens, 15 row moves across its extent, 10 real queued cell saves, and 3 report editor opens/closes.
- All loaded row counts matched 25/50/100.
- 90 intended saves produced exactly 90 additional report revisions; every saved trait was read back and checked. Opening/closing unchanged editors did not add revisions.
- Zero GroupSnapshot, GroupSnapshotMember, ComparisonSet or ComparisonMember rows were created by measurement.
- No pending queue cells remained after each group.
- Stopwatch around Access COM Eval, including automation overhead. Native forms and services were exercised, without physical clicks or screen/display timing.
- One sequential Access session on one local machine, with synthetic observed drafts, no approval routes and no existing prior evaluations. These are small repeated samples, not p95, production-scale, multi-machine or cold-start guarantees. Full raw timings and method are in SCALE_R102_RESULTS.json. Separate EVAL profile used another scratch copy/session.
