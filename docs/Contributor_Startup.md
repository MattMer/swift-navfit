# Choose your contributor

Every launch of an initialized workspace opens **Who is contributing today?**
No name is selected automatically, even if someone used the file previously.

1. Choose your existing name and select **Continue as selected contributor**.
2. If your name is absent, enter it under **Or add your name as a new contributor** and select **Add and continue**. Names must contain 1–120 characters and must not duplicate an existing contributor.
3. Confirm **Contributor: your name** in the workspace context before entering work. New saves, notes and review decisions use that contributor; earlier history keeps its original authors.

**Exit Swift NAVFIT** closes Access without choosing a person. First-time setup
still asks for the command and your name; that explicit choice starts your
session. **Exit without setting up** leaves the blank workspace empty.

After importing an older workspace, close and reopen the destination as directed.
Choose from the imported contributor list. Importing does not confirm the source's
previously active person as the current contributor, and it does not change
historical authorship.

To change contributors during a session, finish or discard pending report and
comparison edits, close the editor, and use the contributor controls in **My Work**.
Reopening the application always asks again. Recovery remains associated with its
original contributor; choosing another person does not transfer their private drafts.

Contributor selection records local attribution. It is not authentication or
proof of identity, and it does not replace the workspace folder's permissions.

This procedure supplements the existing [user guide](Swift_NAVFIT_User_Guide.md).
